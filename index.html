About

Research that bridges user needs and business goals.

4+ Years in UX research

Over the past several years, I've driven and managed high-impact UX research projects for major commercial and governmental organizations across Poland and the Middle East. My experience spans the full product lifecycle - from early-stage discovery to product redesign and usability optimization.

My educational foundation is a bit unconventional for the field: a Bachelor's in Cognitive Science, a Master's in Creative Management, and a technical IT background. That combination gives me a genuine grip on both why people behave the way they do and what's actually feasible to build. As a certified Design Thinking Moderator, I design and facilitate workshops that turn research findings into aligned product strategy.

I also integrate AI tools deliberately and safely into my research workflow - to synthesize faster, spot patterns earlier, and spend more time on the parts of research that actually need a human, like product strategy.

I try to sit close to the user and act as their advocate - while staying realistic about business goals and helping teams find a workable path between the two. Whatever the constraints, the needs and problems of real people stay at the core of the solution.

Featured - R&D Projects

Tattoo industry brand
📱

Building a new app from scratch

A brand in the tattoo industry wanted to launch an entirely new app and needed to validate what to build before design work started.

  1. Discovery interviews to map development areas, user needs, and user segments
  2. Defined product values and user stories together with the team
  3. Tested a mid-fidelity prototype, working closely with the UX design team on research outcomes and use cases
  4. Synthesized and documented findings, feeding them into a further prototype iteration
  5. Ran final usability testing on the resulting high-fidelity prototype

Outcome: because most usability issues had already surfaced and been fixed earlier in the process, the final round of testing landed as a strong success - most user needs and pain points were already resolved before launch.

E-commerce (multiple clients)
🛒

Usability research for e-commerce redesigns

Ran this type of engagement multiple times for different e-commerce clients - usability testing centered on information architecture, findability, and the final stages of the purchase journey, aimed at lifting conversion.

  1. Kick-off meeting with the client to set project goals, recruitment criteria, and timeline
  2. Recruited real or potential users matching the agreed criteria
  3. Conducted usability testing sessions directly on the live website
  4. Analyzed the outcomes and synthesized findings across sessions
  5. Delivered a clean, precise report covering user problems and needs alongside clear recommendations

Outcome: across multiple engagements, this consistently surfaced concrete information-architecture and checkout-flow issues, giving each client a clear, prioritized path to improve findability and conversion.

Mapping 40+ user journeys for a governmental platform

Acted as a research point of contact for Arabic-speaking researchers on a large governmental platform, stepping in wherever support was needed, while owning the platform's central user-journey mapping.
